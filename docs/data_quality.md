### Section 3 states the minimum installment in two different ways

*Check: Installment rules in the policy manual. Severity: medium.*

- payment table: credit card up to 12x, minimum installment R$ 100,00
- section 3.1, 3x: sem valor mínimo de parcela (exceto abaixo de R$ 50,00).
- section 3.1, 4x a 6x: parcela mínima de R$ 80,00.
- section 3.1, 7x a 12x: parcela mínima de R$ 100,00.

**How the agent handles it:** The detailed rules in 3.1 apply (R$ 50 up to 3x, R$ 80 from 4x to 6x, R$ 100 from 7x to 12x), computed by a tool. "Sem valor mínimo (exceto abaixo de R$ 50,00)" is read as a R$ 50 minimum installment.

**Recommendation to the store:** Align the payment table with section 3.1 and reword the 3x rule.
